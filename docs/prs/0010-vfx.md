# PR #10: Visual effects: particles, zones, telegraphs, hit feedback

Branch: `vfx`

## Summary

## Checks

Makes abilities readable. All effects are presentation-only (they read sim state and events; the
sim never sees them, and their randomness is Math.random, not the sim RNG).

- `render/particles.js`: GPU particles, one instanced draw; the CPU writes a spawn record into a
  ring buffer (only the touched range is uploaded), the shader animates drag, gravity, size and fade.
  Capacity scales with quality (3000 at high).
- `render/fx.js`: emitters for every sim event (damage by type, heals, shields, deaths, casts in the
  hero's accent colour, blinks and rewind, impacts, roots, level-ups, tower collapse, whale roll,
  relics) and all named hero/item effects; continuous stun stars, Saffi's wick and Blaze, dash dust,
  honey stings, projectile trails; hero hit flash; camera shake on big hits, rolls and structure deaths.
- `render/zones.js`: one ribbon mesh for Vesper's strokes, loops and walls (dark ink with glowing
  edges, alpha-blended so it reads as ink, not light) and Saffi's fire trail; one instanced disc mesh
  for honey pools, Morrow's clock echoes, relics and telegraphs; pooled honeycomb Hive Domes.
  Telegraphs (Gus's lob and Avalanche) fill until impact and use the caster team's colour.
- `?heroes=a,b,c,d,e,f` sets the roster (used for staged screenshots now, for the lobby later).

Found in review: the first ribbon version would have drawn a faint bridge between separate strokes;
the dome, trail and honey were toned down after the first screenshot washed out the fight.
